How to read this calibration signal, not a ranking

A positive residual means players found the map harder than the estimate shown (they voted “harder”); a negative one means it felt easier. Votes are weighted by accuracy (peak at 96%), and per-map rows are intentionally not published — the private admin view is the only place with map-level detail.
